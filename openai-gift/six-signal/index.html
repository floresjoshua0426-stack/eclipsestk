02 / A sound nobody had to license

The original Six Signal.

This short, original six-note sound is generated locally using mathematics—not taken from any artist’s catalog. Download the actual 32-bit float stereo WAV and inspect the file yourself. Browser playback may resample or convert it for your device’s speakers.

SIX SIGNAL / 6-second WAV

48,000 samples/sec · 2 channels · IEEE Float32 · 6.00 seconds · generated entirely on your device

Nothing generated. No audio plays until you press Listen.
Important technical boundary

Source precision ≠ output precision

The WAV is verifiably IEEE float32 in the file. The final audio route—Safari, Web Audio, Bluetooth, DAC, headphones—is controlled by the browser and device. This demo cannot measure their delivered bit depth.

“Airplane mode” means using files and an offline page already saved before disconnecting; it is not streaming from the internet while disconnected.

You can download this audio to Files or your computer. The source audio is generated here rather than fetched from ECLIPSE or any third-party server.
