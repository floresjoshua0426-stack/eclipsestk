04 · Everything clear

A little clarity.

Studio status: You can design, preview on your hand, and download your artwork today. Photoreal AI rendering, fulfillment, pricing, and payments are not connected yet. We won't collect an order or charge you for something we can't deliver.

Does quantity 1 mean one nail or a full set?

One complete set means 10 press-on nails. The exact contents of a future physical order will be confirmed before checkout.

Can I see the nails on my hand?

Yes. Select “My hand,” upload your own photo, and drag the 5 nail overlays into place. Use the size controls and save a try-on PNG. It is only an approximate styling preview—not a nail-sizing measurement or AI photo edit.

Are these designs made by photoreal AI?

The instant designer produces digital artwork using local browser illustration techniques. Photoreal image generation will require a separately deployed, privacy-conscious AI service and is not active in this early-access version.

Can I order a set today?

Not through this studio yet. Save your concept and ask your chosen nail artist for feasibility, sizing, price, and timing. Breezy's Beauty Studio remains a completely separate business with its own ordering system.

What happens to my hand photo?

It stays in your device's browser memory for the current preview and is not sent to ECLIPSE or the AI. Closing or refreshing the page clears the photo. Saving a try-on downloads a file to your own device.

Will the 3D charms look exactly like this?

Digital art may depict charms, gems and textures that require special materials or extra labor. A human maker must confirm physical feasibility, inventory, sizing, and pricing before producing a set.
